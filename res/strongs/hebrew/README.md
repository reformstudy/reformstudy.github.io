# Strong's Hebrew Lexicon

`hebrew.json` is generated by `npm run ingest:strongs` from STEPBible's TBESH lexicon ([STEPBible/STEPBible-Data](https://github.com/STEPBible/STEPBible-Data), pinned to a commit), maintained by Tyndale House, Cambridge. Do not edit it by hand.

Each entry is keyed by its Strong's number and has the Hebrew word, a transliteration, the part of speech and a one-line gloss.

**Definitions are left out for now.** TBESH's own notes say its definitions are "based on the Abridged BDB by Online Bible, © Larry Pierce of OnlineBible.net" and that "Permission should be gained from Online Bible before these are applied in any project" (olbsupport@onlinebible.net). They come back once permission is given: remove `definitions: false` from the Hebrew entry in `scripts/ingest/strongs.js` and rerun the ingest. Authentication counts them as "definitions left out (Online Bible permission needed)", and fails if one is present while they're withheld.
